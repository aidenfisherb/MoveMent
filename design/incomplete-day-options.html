MoveMent · incomplete days

Where should the “incomplete” label go?

A past day gets the label when lunch or dinner has nothing logged, and you can clear it with a tap. It's information only: no numbers change and nothing asks you to fill the day in. The example day is Wed, Nov 11, with only breakfast and a snack logged. Pick any combination.
