Do and don't

Do
  • True black page, thin gray outlines
  • One orange focal point per screen
  • Monospace for every number, with units
  • Dials for anything with a target
  • Compact rows, pill buttons, black text on orange
Don't
  • Filled gray cards, shadows, gradients, blur
  • A second accent color
  • White text on orange
  • Condensed, all-caps, or serif headings
  • Emoji as icons, big empty hero sections
